# Roadmap

A monocentric closed city with an amenity *gradient* (not a spaceless *A*) would make closed-city rents move even under separable quality of life, because the bid-rent envelope shifts. That is a different object from `UE-H-01` as keyed and should not be silently substituted.

Owner-occupier accounting (mixed residual claimant) is the natural next executable for distractor J, provided it does not relabel the open-city pin.
