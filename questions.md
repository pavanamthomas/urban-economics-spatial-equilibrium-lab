# Open questions on the two cities

The assessment corpus item is in brackets.

Who is the residual claimant after an amenity shock? Households in the closed city; land in the open city (`UE-H-01`, `amenity_shock`). I still do not have mixed owner-occupiers as an executable, only as a distractor.

Why do open-city rents rise more here? In-migration on an upward-sloping *Hˢ*. Closed *N* cannot bid as far.

Why is rent bill 0.50 → 0.60 in the closed city an identity? *R H = Ny/2* under Cobb-Douglas half-shares. That pin is independent of solving the quadratic for *R*.

Why is *R'(d) = −t/q*? Differentiate the budget at optimal *q* (`UE-M-01`). *t* = 0 flattens the gradient. A monocentric *amenity* gradient is a different object and is not this envelope.

Can wages alone rank productivity? No. The disamenity city has a higher wage than the amenity city in the atlas (`UE-E-02`). Sorting by *w* is a negative control I keep around because I already made that mistake once.

Where does a housing demand shock go when *e_s* = 0? Entirely into price (`UE-H-02`, `UE-E-03`).

Ten new jobs, six migrants? Place employment +10, stayer employment +4. Land rent can still rise (`UE-E-01`). Place-based jobs are not stayer jobs.

Is "voting with feet" Tiebout? Only if mobility, many jurisdictions, no spillovers, no scale economies, full information (`UE-M-02`). Mobility alone is a slogan.

House prices on test scores? Constructed OVB: true MWTP for scores is 0; OLS on scores is the park (`UE-H-03`).
