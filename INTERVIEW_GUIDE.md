# Interview map

The assessment corpus item is in brackets.

1. Who is the residual claimant after an amenity shock? Households in the closed city; land in the open city (`UE-H-01`, `amenity_shock`).
2. Why do open-city rents rise more here? In-migration on an upward-sloping *Hˢ*. Closed *N* cannot bid as far.
3. Why is rent bill 0.50 → 0.60 in the closed city an identity? *R H = Ny/2* under Cobb-Douglas half-shares.
4. Why is *R'(d) = −t/q*? Differentiate the budget at optimal *q* (`UE-M-01`). *t* = 0 flattens the gradient.
5. Can wages alone rank productivity? No. Disamenity city has a higher wage than the amenity city in the atlas (`UE-E-02`).
6. Where does a housing demand shock go when *e_s* = 0? Entirely into price (`UE-H-02`, `UE-E-03`).
7. Ten new jobs, six migrants? Place employment +10, stayer employment +4. Land rent can still rise (`UE-E-01`).
8. Is "voting with feet" Tiebout? Only if mobility, many jurisdictions, no spillovers, no scale economies, full information (`UE-M-02`).
9. House prices on test scores? Constructed OVB: true MWTP for scores is 0; OLS on scores is the park (`UE-H-03`).
